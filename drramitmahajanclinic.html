About the Doctor

A Decade of
Expert Care

Dr. Ramit Mahajan is Professor of Gastroenterology and Hepatology who brings over a decade of clinical expertise and research experience to patient care. He has made significant contributions to the field through his work in inflammatory bowel disease, fecal microbiota transplantation (FMT), and liver failure management, with his research published in leading medical journals including the New England Journal of Medicine.

Dr. Mahajan's approach to medicine emphasizes combining evidence-based care with personalized treatment strategies. His clinical expertise spans a comprehensive range of gastroenterological conditions — from complex IBD cases requiring advanced therapeutics to critical liver failure management with specialized interventions like plasmapheresis.

What distinguishes Dr. Mahajan's practice is his commitment to staying at the forefront of medical advances while maintaining a patient-centered focus. Whether managing functional disorders, performing advanced endoscopic procedures, or treating acute liver failure, he ensures each patient receives individualized care based on the latest scientific evidence.
